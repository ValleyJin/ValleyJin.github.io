I rebuilt this site from scratch. The old version was a stock theme with placeholder
posts; this one is meant to be lived in.

Two ideas drive the redesign:

**The homepage should never be stale.** A scheduled job reads my public GitHub activity
every day — recent repositories, language mix, contribution count — and writes it into the
page. I don't have to remember to update anything; the "Currently building" section is
always current.

**Writing should feel good to do.** The layout is deliberately restrained: near-monochrome,
one accent color, a serif reading column. I can open a small editor, write, and hit publish
from anywhere — phone included — and the post appears here minutes later.

> The best system for writing daily is the one that gets out of your way.

If you're reading this, the machine works. More soon — on serving LLMs cheaply, on L2
infrastructure, and on what it takes to give robots a usable model of the world.
